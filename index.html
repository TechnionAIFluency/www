Overview

What the course is

AI Fluency and Agentic Methods for Engineers prepares students to practice their discipline alongside AI agents, treating an agent as a component of engineering workflows.

Every graduate ships one working agentic system that solves a real problem in their own field, delivered in six milestones on a two-week beat. The syllabus has the full description; the schedule has the dates.

The spine

Three postures

  • A — ask a model. You pose a question and judge the answer by eye.
  • B — write a script. Repeatable, but it cannot adapt to what it finds.
  • C — a model over a tested ground. The model proposes, the ground computes, the model reads and adjusts. Every project here is C.

The failure test runs all semester: delete the LLM, drop in a for loop. If it does as well, you built B behind a chat window.
