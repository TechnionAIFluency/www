Thirteen weeks

Each week is a two-hour lecture and a two-hour exercise. Week 12 is open in lecture content by design; its exercise half is reserved for project build and the red-team exchange.

The beat. Milestones fall due in weeks 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12, and each is reviewed in the Success and Failure gallery the week after. Week 13 has nothing due: it carries the showcase and the defence audit.

Week 12. Deliberately open: a December decision picks a topic, never a format. The exercise half is reserved for project build and the red-team exchange, so the structure is fixed even while the content is not.

The revision gate. Week 6 is the revision gate. A ground that fails criterion 4 or criterion 6 is pivoted there, in the pivot clinic, with a TA's approval. Later is too late: P6 falls due in week 12, so a ground adopted after week 6 has no weeks left to carry a project onto it.

Every week

The fixed session structure

Both halves of every week carry standing slots. They are not topics and spend no topic slot: this is what a session looks like before any content is placed in it.

Lecture — the fixed slots inside the 120 minutes.
Slot Length What it is
Field update 10 min What moved in the field this week. Part of the deliberately open fifth of the syllabus.
Exercise — the fixed slots inside the 120 minutes.
Slot Length What it is
Standup 10 min Three students, two minutes each, screen-shared: where they are, what broke. This is what surfaces the gallery material.
Your project 100 min This week's capability applied to your own project.
Commit and disclose 10 min Commit, push, one line in docs/disclosure.md; milestone checks run in CI.

The Success and Failure gallery is not a weekly slot. It has its own session in weeks 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11 — the week after each milestone falls due — and week 13's showcase carries P6's. Successes are named and credited; failures are anonymised.
