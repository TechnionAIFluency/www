Lectures

One lecture a week

Each lecture is two hours and opens with a ten-minute field update. Week 12 is open by design: a December decision picks its topic, never its format. Each week links to its deck once that deck is published — a single self-contained file.

  • The 10× engineer

  • The for-loop test: delete the LLM, does a grid search do as well?

  • The environment: WSL, the harnesses, uv, git, the Linux terminal
  • git concepts
  • The syllabus, and the final project
